Our story

More than hair — it's a feeling.

Emmi & Co is a happy, vibey, luxury salon in Benoni Rynfield, founded in 2026 by Ashley Bayne.

Meet Ashley

Founded on personal connection.

Hi, I'm Ashley — the founder of Emmi & Co. I built this salon around something simple: every woman deserves to feel beautiful, confident, and genuinely seen.

From the music to the lighting to the way we hand you your coffee, every detail is intentional. We don't just do hair; we create a moment in your week that's just for you.

What we believe

Luxury, with warmth.

What makes us different is the balance — high-quality hair in a relaxed, welcoming environment that feels like your safe space, not just another salon.

Personal

One-on-one attention, every appointment. We get to know you, your hair, and your lifestyle.

Modern

Effortless, on-trend hair that suits real life — not just the salon mirror.

Feminine

An elevated, beautifully designed space crafted to feel calm and uplifting.

Detailed

From the consultation to the final spritz, every detail matters to us.

Caring

Comfort first. We listen, we adapt, we treat you like a friend.

Community

The space

Designed to feel like a deep breath.

A clean, modern interior in soft greys, beige, and white — accented with little pops of colour and personality. Comfortable seating, a calm atmosphere, and great lighting (because nothing beats a perfect-light selfie after your appointment).

Whether you're in for a quick blow-wave or a full balayage transformation, we want every visit to feel like a small luxury.
